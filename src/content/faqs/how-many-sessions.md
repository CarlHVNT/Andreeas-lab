question: "How many sessions will I need?"
order: 3
category: treatment
---

It depends on what you want to change and where you are starting from. A drainage programme is often six to ten sessions. Skin firming and body remodelling usually need ten to twelve. Recovery after surgery follows your healing.

Andreea gives you a number at your consultation and writes it into your plan. If you reach your goal sooner, you stop sooner.
