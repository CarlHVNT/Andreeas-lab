question: "Who should not have Icoone?"
order: 6
category: treatment
needsConfirmation: true
---

Icoone is not suitable during pregnancy, with an active infection or fever, with blood clots or a history of thrombosis, or during active cancer treatment. Some heart, kidney and skin conditions also rule it out, and recent surgery needs your surgeon’s clearance first.

This is a general list, not a diagnosis. Andreea goes through your medical history at the consultation and will tell you plainly if Icoone is not right for you. When in doubt, ask your doctor first.
