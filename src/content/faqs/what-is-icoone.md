question: "What is Icoone?"
order: 1
category: treatment
---

Icoone is a non-invasive body and face treatment developed in Italy. A handpiece with small rollers moves over the skin and gently stimulates the tissue beneath it, many times a second. That helps fluid drain, softens tight or fibrous areas and encourages the skin to firm.

It is not a massage, although it can feel like one, and it is not a machine that does the work by itself. The result depends on how the session is planned and performed, which is why Andreea does every session herself.
