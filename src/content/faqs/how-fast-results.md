question: "How quickly will I see results?"
order: 4
category: treatment
---

Most people feel lighter and less puffy after the first drainage session, and that feeling is real. Changes in skin texture and shape take longer, usually several sessions, because the tissue needs repeated stimulation to respond.

Results vary from person to person, and Andreea will not promise you a specific outcome. She will tell you what is realistic for you, and she measures as you go so you can see what is changing.
