Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to move or cancel a session, by WhatsApp. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, are charged in full, because that time was held for you and cannot be offered to someone else. If you are unwell, tell Andreea as early as you can and she will do her best to be flexible.
